# Morpho Support

Need help with Morpho: Content Hub? Use the developer’s email listed in [Contact Information](https://github.com/mabdelhafizstudio/Morpho-Privacy-Policy/blob/main/README.md#2-contact).

Include a description of the problem, your device model, operating-system version, and Morpho version. A screenshot or exact error message can help. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, or private source URLs; redact them from screenshots and logs.

## Connection and playback help

Check that your media server or connected service is reachable, your connection details are current, and you have permission to access the selected media. For playback problems, include the file format and whether the issue happens with one title or multiple titles.

## Privacy and account help

For privacy, account, or deletion questions, use the same [published contact information](https://github.com/mabdelhafizstudio/Morpho-Privacy-Policy/blob/main/README.md#2-contact). Read the [Privacy Policy](README.md) for data-handling details.

Deleting a Morpho account does not delete your Apple account, your accounts with other providers, or original media on those services.
